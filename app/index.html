Sources & methods

Where the numbers come from

  • BPS — Statistics Indonesia (bps.go.id): GDP and growth, consumer prices, unemployment (Sakernas), poverty, population census and estimates, HDI, Gini ratio. Every headline figure on this page is a published BPS aggregate.
  • Bank Indonesia (bi.go.id): the BI policy rate.
  • United Nations population estimates: the median-age figure, labelled approximate.

How to read this site

  • Figures marked approx. are rounded or estimated from published aggregates; everything else is the official published value for the year shown.
  • Unemployment is the August open unemployment rate; poverty is the September rate; inflation is the December year-on-year rate — each series uses the same month every year so the years compare like with like.
  • Provincial populations are from the 2020 Population Census, the latest census; the 2024 national total is the BPS mid-year estimate.
  • This site is a static page: it makes no network requests and works offline from its own files.
